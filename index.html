COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Find answers to the most common questions about our programs and lectures.

What courses do you currently offer?

We specialized in Engineering education, offering comprehensive coaching for B.Tech subjects, GATE, KEAM, and various Technical PSC exams. Each curriculum is designed by alumni from top-tier technical universities.

Are the classes live or can I watch recordings later?

We offer both! Our live interactive sessions allow you to clear doubts in real-time. For flexibility, every live session is recorded and made available in your dashboard, so you can learn at your own pace anytime.

How can I access the study materials?

Once you enroll in a course, you'll receive login credentials for our student portal. From there, you can download curated study notes, access previous year question banks, and take mock tests.

Do you provide certificates after course completion?

Yes, we provide a certificate of completion for all our professional and vocational courses. These certificates can be used to showcase your technical proficiency to potential employers.

Can I talk to a mentor directly for doubt clearing?

Absolutely. We follow a mentorship-first approach. Every batch has a dedicated technical mentor, and you can reach out via our platform or the official WhatsApp support channel for one-on-one assistance.
