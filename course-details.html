Course Description

This premium course bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical manufacturing applications required in standard engineering boards. Join Franklin Josemon M as he breaks down complex subjects with his unique, engaging teaching style. You will receive access to digital study materials, an interactive student community, and one-on-one doubt cleaning sessions before university exams.

Syllabus Timeline

Module 1: Introduction to Manufacturing

Deep dive into traditional manufacturing methods, history, material properties, and basic machinery tools including lathe and milling machine operations.

Module 2: Computer Integrated Manufacturing

Understanding CAD, CAM, CIM. Basics of numerical controls (NC) and computer numerical controls (CNC) part programming fundamentals.

Module 3: Advanced Material Sciences

Properties of super alloys, composites, ceramics. Introduction to powder metallurgy and nano-manufacturing.
